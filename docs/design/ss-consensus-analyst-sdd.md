## 1. Agent Overview

Given every evaluator's findings and ratings, identifies disagreements (rating spread, conflicting or unique findings), proposes a consensus rating per offeror and factor with rationale tied to specific findings, and lists questions for the SSEB Chair.

### In Scope

Disagreement detection, proposed consensus rating and narrative, Chair question list.

### Out of Scope

Assigning ratings, making decisions, contacting offerors, using information outside the provided record.

### Assumptions

Proposals and plans are synthetic; model selected by the AI Trust Layer default.

## 2. Agent Framework

Low-code agent. **Input:** `solicitationId`, `evaluationSet` (JSON from CompileEvaluations), `scales` (JSON). **Output:** `consensusDraft` (JSON string: per offeror × factor proposedRating, rationale, supportingFindingIds, questions), `disagreementCount` (integer). System prompt intent: act as a federal source selection support analyst; use only provided content and tool results; cite every claim; never rate or decide; output strict JSON.

## 5. Evaluation Criteria

### Success Metrics

100% of spread ≥ 2 pairs flagged; proposed ratings only from the plan scale; every rationale cites ≥ 1 finding id.

### Trajectory Evaluation

Tool calls within policy limits.

### LLM-Judge Criteria

Specific, factual, cites evidence, no rating or decision language attributed to the agent.
